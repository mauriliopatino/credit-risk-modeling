# Maurilio Patiño García

Credit risk executive and hands-on quant, with 30 years in banking and insurance across Mexico and Latin America. I have led full risk functions while personally building the models beneath them: origination and behavioral scoring, PD-LGD-EAD estimation, transition matrices, survival models, copulas, Monte Carlo methods, and machine learning applied to credit.

## What you will find here

- **[credit-risk-modeling](https://github.com/mauriliopatino/credit-risk-modeling)**: a practitioner's series of technical notes and Python notebooks on credit portfolio risk, from the mathematical foundations of the Basel IRB formula (ASRF, the Gaussian copula inside it) to simulation, risk measures, and what happens when the dependence structure is misspecified. Each piece includes both the notebook and a readable PDF.

More notebooks are on the way: transition and generator matrices, survival analysis for credit behavior, and empirical PD-LGD correlation with agency loan-level data.
